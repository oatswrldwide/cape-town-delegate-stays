# Rooibos and Tea, from South African Origin

## A tea that only grows here

Rooibos (Aspalathus linearis) grows exclusively in the Cederberg mountain region of South Africa's Western Cape. The combination of sandstone soils, winter rainfall and Mediterranean climate cannot be replicated elsewhere. For buyers, this means genuine origin protection and a story that resonates in wellness, premium and specialty tea markets.

---

## What We Can Source

### Rooibos Formats
- **Traditional / Fermented** — The classic amber-red rooibos with naturally sweet, nutty notes
- **Green (Unfermented)** — Higher antioxidant content, lighter colour, more delicate flavour
- **Organic Certified** — EU organic, USDA NOP, JAS and Bio Suisse compliant options
- **Extracts & Concentrates** — For beverage manufacturing and cosmetic applications

### Packaging Options
- Bulk loose leaf in 10kg–20kg cartons or woven bags
- Retail packs: 20–100 count tea bags, 50g–250g loose leaf boxes
- Foodservice: bulk filter bags, catering packs
- Private-label: custom packaging, blending and branding support

### Other South African Teas
- Honeybush (Cyclopia spp.) — floral, caffeine-free, also endemic to the Cape
- Buchu — traditional medicinal herb, growing interest in functional beverages
- Indigenous blends — rooibos combined with local botanicals

---

## Sourcing Considerations

**Seasonality:** Rooibos is harvested in summer (January–March). Green rooibos has a shorter processing window. Planning enquiries in Q3–Q4 for the following year's supply is advisable.

**Grading:** Cut length, colour uniformity and dust content determine grade. We help you match grade to application — premium retail requires different specifications than bulk ingredient use.

**Certification:** Many Cederberg producers hold organic, Fairtrade and Rainforest Alliance certification. We verify current certificate validity before introduction.

**Regulatory:** Rooibos enjoys Geographical Indication (GI) status in South Africa and is recognised as a distinctive product in the EU and other markets. We ensure documentation supports origin claims.

# Fresh Apples for International Markets

## From Elgin and Grabouw to your market

South Africa's apple industry is concentrated in the cool, mountainous valleys of the Western Cape — primarily Elgin, Grabouw and the Langkloof. These regions produce apples with excellent colour, firmness and sugar-acid balance, supported by modern packhouse infrastructure and established cold chain logistics.

South Africa is a counter-seasonal supplier to the Northern Hemisphere, with harvest running from February through August depending on variety. This timing creates natural opportunities for buyers looking to extend their apple programme beyond domestic seasons.

---

## Varieties Available for Export

| Variety | Harvest Window | Profile | Key Markets |
|---------|---------------|---------|-------------|
| **Granny Smith** | March–May | Bright green, tart, excellent storage | Europe, Asia, Middle East |
| **Royal Gala** | February–April | Sweet, crisp, striped red-orange | UK, Europe, Southeast Asia |
| **Pink Lady** | April–May | Firm, sweet-tart, pink blush | Premium Europe, Asia |
| **Golden Delicious** | March–April | Sweet, mellow, versatile | Africa, Middle East, Asia |
| **Fuji** | April–May | Very sweet, dense, long shelf-life | Asia, Middle East |
| **Topred / Starking** | March–April | Deep red, sweet | Europe, Russia, Middle East |

---

## Export Formats & Specifications

- **Class 1 / Extra:** Premium retail specification. Uniform size, minimal blemish, high colour.
- **Class 1:** Standard retail. Good colour, minor skin blemishes permitted.
- **Class 2:** Processing and secondary retail. Suitable for slicing, juicing and manufacturing.

**Packaging:**
- Cartons: 10kg, 12.5kg, 18kg standard export cartons
- Bins: 400kg wooden or plastic bins for processing orders
- Retail-ready: Pre-packed trays, flow-wrap and bagged options

**Sizing:** Count per carton varies by destination preference. Common counts: 100, 113, 125, 138, 150, 163, 175.

---

## Critical Timing & Logistics

**Cold storage:** South African apples are stored in controlled-atmosphere (CA) and ultra-low oxygen (ULO) facilities, extending market availability by several months post-harvest.

**Shipping:** Sea freight from Cape Town to European ports (12–18 days) or Asia (18–25 days). Reefer containers maintain 0.5°C with controlled atmosphere where required.

**Phytosanitary:** South Africa has established protocols with major markets. We ensure inspection, certification and cold treatment requirements are met before shipment.

# Dried Fruit with Room to Build

## South African dried fruit for global shelves and kitchens

South Africa's dried fruit sector processes surplus and processing-grade fresh fruit into stable, export-ready products. The industry is particularly strong in dried peaches, pears, apples and pineapple — with growing capability in mango and mixed fruit blends.

For buyers, South African dried fruit offers competitive pricing, established food safety systems and flexible pack formats from bulk ingredient sacks to consumer-ready retail packs.

---

## Product Range

### Dried Pineapple
- **Rings / Slices:** Standard snack format, sulphured or natural
- **Chunks / Diced:** 5mm–15mm for baking, cereals, trail mixes
- **Cores:** Value-priced option for industrial processing

### Dried Stone Fruit
- **Peach Halves / Slices:** Clingstone and freestone varieties
- **Pear Halves:** Soft texture, popular in baking and snacking
- **Apricots:** Limited volumes, premium positioning

### Dried Apple
- **Rings:** Snacking and baking
- **Diced:** 5mm–10mm for manufacturing
- **Flakes:** For cereals and muesli

### Mixed Fruit
- Custom blends for retail muesli, baking and industrial use
- Ratio and fruit type tailored to specification

---

## Processing & Specification

**Sulphur dioxide (SO₂):** Most South African dried fruit is sulphured to preserve colour and extend shelf life. Natural (unsulphured) options are available but with shorter shelf life and darker colour. We clarify SO₂ levels (typically 600–2000 ppm depending on product and destination) to match your regulatory limits.

**Moisture content:** Typically 15–22% depending on application. Lower moisture for long shelf-life retail; higher moisture for soft-eating snack formats.

**Packaging:**
- Bulk: 10kg–12.5kg cartons or poly-lined boxes
- Retail: 100g–1kg stand-up pouches, pillow bags, tubs
- Industrial: 25kg poly-lined sacks

**Certification:** HACCP, BRC and ISO 22000 are common. Organic dried fruit is available in limited volumes.

---

## Applications We Support

- **Retail snacking:** Private-label dried fruit lines, trail mix components
- **Baking & manufacturing:** Diced fruit for muffins, cereals, energy bars
- **Foodservice:** Bulk packs for hospitality and catering
- **Ingredient supply:** Consistent specification for food manufacturers

# Nut Supply for the Next Stage of Your Business

## South Africa's macadamia and pecan advantage

South Africa is the world's largest producer of macadamia nuts outside of Australia, with orchards concentrated in Mpumalanga, Limpopo and the KwaZulu-Natal Midlands. The industry has matured rapidly over the past decade, with modern cracking facilities, strict grading protocols and growing value-added capability.

Pecan production is smaller but expanding, particularly in the Northern Cape and Orange River regions.

For international buyers, this means access to high-quality kernel with competitive pricing, established food safety certification and flexible processing options.

---

## Macadamia Nuts

### Kernel Styles
- **Style 0:** Large whole kernel (21mm+), premium snack retail
- **Style 1:** Whole kernel (18–21mm), standard retail and gift
- **Style 2:** Large halves, baking and manufacturing
- **Style 3:** Halves and pieces, ice cream and confectionery
- **Style 4:** Small pieces, cereal and bar manufacturing
- **Style 5:** Chips and fines, industrial and oil extraction
- **Style 6:** Large chips, cookie and biscuit applications
- **Style 7:** Meal / flour, gluten-free baking and coatings
- **Style 8:** Natural wholes and halves, in-shell for roasting

### Processing Options
- **Raw / Natural:** Dried and cracked, not roasted
- **Dry Roasted:** No oil added, salt or unsalted
- **Oil Roasted:** Crisper texture, longer shelf life
- **Chocolate-coated / Flavoured:** Value-added for confectionery

### Sizing & Grades
- **Supreme:** Premium colour, minimal blemish
- **Grade 1 / Standard S:** Good colour, standard snack grade
- **Grade 2:** Slight colour variation, suitable for manufacturing
- **Grade 3 / Utility:** Deeper colour, industrial use

---

## Pecan Nuts

- **Halves:** Premium snacking and baking
- **Pieces:** Manufacturing, praline, ice cream
- **Meal / Flour:** Gluten-free applications

South African pecans are typically harvested in May–July, with shelling running through September.

---

## Packaging & Export

- **Vacuum packs:** 11.34kg (25lb) cartons — industry standard for kernel
- **Bulk sacks:** 25kg for industrial users
- **Retail:** 100g–1kg stand-up pouches, jars, tins
- **In-shell:** 10kg–25kg nets or cartons for roasting operations

**Certification:** Most major processors hold HACCP, BRC, ISO 22000 and Kosher/Halal certification. Organic macadamia is available from select growers.

**Seasonality:** Macadamia harvest runs March–September. Early planning (Q4 prior year) secures better allocation from top processors.

South Africa's wine industry dates back to 1659, yet it is one of the most technically progressive in the New World. The combination of ancient soils, diverse mesoclimates and sustained investment in winemaking education produces wines that compete on quality at every price point.

For international buyers, South Africa offers exceptional value, strong sustainability credentials (through the industry-wide Integrated Production of Wine system), and increasing availability of premium and super-premium wines for discerning markets.

---

- South Africa's most famous wine address
- Cabernet Sauvignon, Merlot, Bordeaux-style blends

- Strong co-operative and private cellar infrastructure

- Boutique wineries, high-end tourism influence

**Labelling:** We ensure compliance with destination market requirements — health warnings, alcohol percentage, allergen statements and importer details.

**Sustainability:** The IPW (Integrated Production of Wine) scheme covers over 95% of South African vineyards. Many producers also hold WIETA (ethical trade) and Biodiversity & Wine Initiative accreditation.
